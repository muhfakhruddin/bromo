About BROMO

From mathematical research to computational exploration.

BROMO stands for Biomathematical Research on Modeling & Optimization. It is an evolving research platform for developing, organizing, and communicating mathematical and computational research on biological systems.

BROMO focuses particularly on systems in which biological processes interact with environmental, spatial, temporal, and human-related factors.

The platform provides a bridge between scientific publications and computational exploration by translating research questions, mathematical models, data analyses, and simulations into accessible research products.

02
Research Focus

Understanding dynamic biological systems through mathematics.

BROMO brings together mathematical modeling, statistical analysis, computational methods, and optimization to investigate complex biological and environmental processes.

MODELING

Mathematical Modeling

Development and analysis of mathematical models for describing biological processes, transmission dynamics, interactions between populations, and other dynamic systems.

DATA

Data-Driven Modeling

Integration of observational data with statistical and computational methods to investigate temporal, spatial, environmental, and epidemiological patterns.

COMPUTATION

Computational Exploration

Numerical simulation, forecasting, spatial analysis, visualization, and computational experimentation for exploring model behavior and research questions.

OPTIMIZATION

Modeling & Optimization

Computational approaches for parameter estimation, model improvement, decision analysis, and optimization within biological and environmental systems.
