Intermittent Time Series Modeling

Explore sparse and zero-heavy dengue incidence patterns at finer spatial scales using computational approaches designed for intermittent time series.

Dengue Intermittent Time Series Finer Spatial Scale
01
Overview

Modeling dengue when observations are sparse and intermittent.

At finer spatial scales, dengue incidence may contain many periods with zero reported cases separated by occasional non-zero observations.

Intermittent time series modeling provides a framework for exploring these sparse temporal patterns while accounting for the distinctive structure of the observed data.

02
Data Characteristics

Understand the temporal structure of sparse observations.

Intermittent dengue data can exhibit long sequences of zero observations, irregular non-zero events, and substantial variation between locations and observation periods.
