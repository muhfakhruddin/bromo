Spatio-Temporal Modeling

Dengue Space Time Mobility
01
Overview

Understanding dengue across space and time.

Dengue dynamics can vary substantially between locations and over time. Spatio-temporal modeling provides a framework for investigating these variations simultaneously.

This research framework is designed to explore how temporal disease dynamics, geographical characteristics, environmental conditions, and interactions between locations may contribute to observed dengue patterns.
