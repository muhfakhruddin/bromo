Host–Vector Model

A mathematical model for exploring dengue transmission dynamics through interactions between human and mosquito populations.

Dengue Host–Vector Temporal
01
Overview

Understanding dengue transmission through host–vector interactions.

The host–vector framework represents dengue transmission as a dynamic interaction between human hosts and mosquito vectors. Changes in one population can influence infection dynamics in the other population over time.

This research product is designed to provide a computational environment for exploring model behavior, evaluating transmission dynamics, and examining model outputs under different data and parameter settings.
