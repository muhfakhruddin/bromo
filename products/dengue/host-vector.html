Dengue Host–Vector SIR–UV Temporal
01
Overview

Understanding dengue transmission through host–vector interactions.

Host–vector models describe dengue transmission as a dynamic interaction between human hosts and mosquito vectors. Infection can move between these populations through mosquito-mediated transmission.

This modeling framework provides a basis for exploring transmission dynamics over time and for investigating how changes in model assumptions, parameters, and data may influence epidemic behavior.

02
Model Structure

SIR–UV host–vector framework.

The model organizes the human population into susceptible, infectious, and recovered compartments, while the mosquito population is represented through susceptible and infectious vector states.
