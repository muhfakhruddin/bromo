About BROMO

From mathematical research to computational exploration.

BROMO — Biomathematical Research on Modeling & Optimization — is a research platform for exploring mathematical and computational approaches to biological systems and their interactions with environmental processes.

The platform connects mathematical modeling, data, computation, and optimization to investigate dynamic processes across epidemiological, ecological, and biological–environmental systems.

BROMO translates research outcomes into accessible computational products, including models, simulations, predictions, spatial analyses, and interactive research prototypes.

Vector-Web

Community observations for vector surveillance.

Vector-Web is a community-oriented research component for collecting, organizing, and visualizing observations related to disease vectors and potential breeding sites.

Observation data can be represented spatially to explore geographical patterns and support the development of data-driven vector surveillance research.

VECTOR-WEB

Community Observation Map

Community reports, observation locations, verification status, and spatial summaries can be integrated into an interactive map.
