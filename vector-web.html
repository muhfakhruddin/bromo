BROMO / Vector-Web
Community Observation Platform

Vector-Web

A community-based research platform for collecting, organizing, and spatially exploring observations of disease vectors and potential breeding sites.

Community Observation Vector Spatial Data Mapping
01
Overview

Connecting community observations with spatial research.

Vector-Web is designed as a community-oriented component of BROMO for documenting observations related to disease vectors, larvae, and potential breeding sites.

Observations can be organized spatially and explored through maps to support research on the distribution and dynamics of vector-related environmental conditions.

Vector-Web is intended to complement mathematical modeling with observational information collected from communities and field activities.

02
Community Observation

Document observations from the field.

Community members and field observers may contribute information about observed vectors, suspected larvae, or environmental locations that may function as potential breeding sites.

REPORT AN OBSERVATION

Community reporting interface

The reporting interface will support observation information such as photographs, observation type, location, date, and relevant field notes.

Reporting Module
03
Observation Map

Explore observations across space.

Submitted observations can be represented on an interactive map to explore their geographical distribution and identify areas with different levels of reporting activity.

INTERACTIVE MAP

Vector-Web Observation Map

Observation locations, spatial summaries, report density, and verification information will be visualized here.

Observation → Location → Verification → Spatial Visualization

Map intensity represents the density of submitted observations and should not be interpreted directly as disease incidence or disease risk.

04
Observation Status

Separate community reports from verified observations.

Community-submitted information should be interpreted according to its verification status. A submitted observation does not automatically represent a confirmed vector identification.

OBSERVATION WORKFLOW

Verification status

Community Report → Pending Verification → Verified Observation

Verification procedures and classification criteria can be developed as the research platform evolves.

05
Spatial Summary

Transform observations into explorable spatial information.

Aggregated observations can be summarized across geographical areas and time periods to investigate patterns in community reporting and vector-related environmental observations.

SPATIAL ANALYTICS

Observation summaries

Summary statistics, temporal patterns, geographical aggregation, observation density, and other spatial indicators will be presented here.

06
Data & Methodology

Transparent interpretation of community-generated data.

Vector-Web data originate from observations and reports that may differ in completeness, spatial precision, and verification status. These limitations should be considered when interpreting maps and analytical outputs.

Methodological documentation will describe data collection, verification procedures, spatial aggregation, privacy protection, analytical methods, and appropriate interpretation of Vector-Web outputs.
