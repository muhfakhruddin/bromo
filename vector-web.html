01
Overview

Community observations as spatial research data.

Vector-Web is designed as a participatory research environment where community observations related to disease vectors and potential breeding sites can be documented and explored spatially.

Submitted observations are not automatically treated as scientifically verified records. Instead, Vector-Web separates community reports from observations that have undergone a verification process.

06
Data & Methodology

Transparent interpretation of community-generated data.

Community-generated observations require careful interpretation. Reporting intensity may vary across locations and periods and should not be interpreted directly as vector abundance, disease incidence, or epidemiological risk.

The methodological framework for Vector-Web will document data collection, verification procedures, spatial aggregation, privacy protection, analytical methods, and limitations of the observation data.
