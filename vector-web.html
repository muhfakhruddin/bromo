01
Overview

Community observations for exploratory vector surveillance.

Vector-Web provides a spatial interface for documenting community reports of suspected disease vectors, larvae, and potential breeding sites.

Areas receiving a larger concentration of reports are represented with stronger map intensity, allowing clusters of suspected vector observations to be explored geographically.

Report density represents community-generated observations and should not be interpreted directly as confirmed vector abundance, disease incidence, or epidemiological risk.

03
Map Interpretation

More reports, stronger map intensity.

Vector-Web aggregates community observations spatially. Locations with relatively few reports appear yellow, while increasing concentrations progress through orange toward red.

LOW Lower Report Density

Relatively fewer suspected vector reports have been submitted in the area.

MODERATE Moderate Report Density

A larger concentration of community observations has been recorded.

HIGH Higher Report Density

A comparatively high concentration of suspected vector reports has been submitted.

06
Data & Methodology

Community-generated data require careful interpretation.

Differences in reporting activity may reflect community participation, population distribution, accessibility, awareness, or other factors in addition to the underlying presence of vectors.

Therefore, areas with high report density should be interpreted as locations with a high concentration of suspected vector reports and not automatically as locations with high vector abundance or high disease transmission risk.
